# The X Pillar Discipline

Pillars keep a week coherent without making it monotone. On X the default mix
leans harder into build-in-public and engagement than LinkedIn does, because the
audience skews toward builders and the ranker pays most for conversation.

## Default pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it is | Ranked goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build-in-Public | 40% | real numbers, progress, mistakes from your own work | Conversation, Follow |
| Insight | 30% | contrarian takes, data points, teaching posts and threads | Send, Read-through |
| Engagement | 20% | replies, value-add quotes, relatable singles | Conversation |
| Promotion | 10% | a launch, an offer, results that imply the product | Send |

Adjust the mix to the user's goal: a builder in launch mode might run 30/30/20/20.
Never let one pillar exceed 60% of the week.

## Why these pillars on X specifically

- **Build-in-Public** over-indexes on X. A real metric (good or ugly) is the
  easiest reply you can earn, and replies are weighted 5 against a like's 0.66.
- **Insight** is where sends live. A hard number or a how-to is what readers
  forward to a colleague (copy-link share 20, DM share 5). Depth that gets
  finished also feeds read-through, which retrieval now trains on.
- **Engagement** is a pillar, not a chore, but know what replies do in the code:
  they are not shown to non-followers in For You, and reach followers at × 0.75.
  Their value is the conversation and the mutual follows it creates, which later
  weight peers' replies on your originals at 20 instead of 5.
- **Promotion** is capped low. A timeline that reads like an ad earns mutes
  (-58.8) and "not interested" (-52.3). Prefer results that imply the product.

## Cadence, from the code

- **1-3 strong originals a day.** For one viewer, your second post in the 48-hour
  window is scored × 0.625, the third × 0.44, flattening at × 0.25. Each extra
  post competes with your best one; post only what would rank first.
- **Originals in the audience's awake hours.** Accounts under 50,000 followers
  get a boost for originals in their first 2 hours (under 200 impressions), so a
  post at 3 a.m. local to the audience wastes it.
- **Nothing lives past 48 hours.** Plan evergreen material as fresh angles, not
  reposts: people who saw a post are filtered from seeing it again, and
  duplicate text is labelled as spam.
- **Depth as one piece.** On Premium, one long post or an Article per week
  usually beats several threads: one original, no reply discount, full dwell.
- **Reply blocks daily.** 5-10 substantive, specific replies a day on accounts in
  your niche. Never the same wording twice.

## Goal balance

Spread the week across the four ranked goals (`../../../references/algorithm-heuristics.md`,
The goals the ranker pays for): Conversation, Send, Follow, Read-through. Likes
and reposts are side effects, and bookmarks carry no ranking weight, so none of
them gets a weekly target.

## Anti-patterns

- A week of near-identical takes (the diversity rerank pushes them down, and
  duplicates are labelled spam).
- More than three originals in a day "to stay visible" (author decay).
- All-promotion week (mutes).
- Zero reply budget (no mutual follows, no conversation).
- Repeating the same formula every day (the pattern becomes visible).
