# Example: 7-Day X Plan

**Theme:** shipping an AI agent in public
**Audience:** indie builders, AI engineers, early founders (US, Eastern time)
**Account:** Premium, 6,400 followers (under 50,000: originals get the 2-hour small-account boost)
**Pillar mix:** 40% Build-in-Public / 30% Insight / 20% Engagement / 10% Promotion
**Cadence:** 1-2 originals/day weekdays, 1 on Saturday, reply block daily

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | AM | single | Insight | X1 One-Liner Contrarian | "most agents fail on retries, not reasoning" | Conversation | 9:30 AM |
| Mon | PM | reply block | Engagement | R1/R2/R3 | 7 builder threads on agents | Conversation | 2:00 PM |

## Daily reply targets

- **Who:** peer builders at 2-20k followers who follow back and talk back (mutual
  follows weight their replies on your originals at 20 instead of 5), AI founders
  shipping in public, and 3-4 larger creators in the niche.
- **Templates:** R1 Answer-Their-Question, R2 Concede-Then-Sharpen, R3
  Extend-Their-Point, R5 Value-Add Quote.
- **Target:** 5-10 substantive replies/day, each written for its post. No "great
  thread!" filler, never the same wording twice, no links in replies to strangers.

## Balance check

- [x] At most 2 originals on any day (author decay makes a third compete with the first two)
- [x] Originals posted in the audience's morning, inside the 2-hour boost window
- [x] At least 1 build-in-public post (Tue, Thu, Sat)
- [x] At least 1 piece of finished depth (Tue long post, Fri Article)
- [x] At least 1 engagement/reply-heavy day (reply blocks Mon, Thu; quote Wed)
- [x] No pillar over 60%
- [x] No formula repeated more than twice
- [x] Goal spread: Conversation (Mon, Wed, Thu), Send (Tue, Wed, Fri PM),
      Read-through (Fri AM), Follow (Sat)

## Notes

- The Fri PM promotion post leans on X10's "results imply the product" shape so
  it does not read as an ad, and lands on a different day from the Tuesday
  teardown so the two do not compete for the same viewers.
- Tuesday and Saturday are long posts, not threads: the account is on Premium,
  and one original avoids the reply discount threads take on tweets 2..N.
- Any product or launch link goes in a reply to the post. Links are not
  penalised by the ranker, but the post should deliver the value before the
  reader leaves.
